## v07 — 2026-10-18

Compared with and based on [v06](v06/Set_09_Fabled_v06.3mf).

- Replaced the thresholded raster trace with explicit closed Bézier contours for all six letters, the A's four-point star, the open-book ornament and the underline. Retained the source artwork's letter shapes and simplified the fine doubled spine hairlines for printing.
- Added a continuous Marine Blue field between the ornaments. The complete book-shaped backing supports every letter and ornament and joins the eyelet through a 7 mm wide neck. The eyelet is now the only through-hole.
- Added continuous nominal 0.50 mm gold borders around the purple letter faces, with two open B counters and one open D counter. Gold rises from 2 to 2.6 mm; purple rises from 2.6 to 3 mm. Colour volumes meet at their faces without overlapping.
- Preserved the v06 hole coordinates and centre exactly: 6.5 mm bore, nominal 10.5 mm outer diameter, 2 mm radial rim and 2 mm backing/loop height. Retained all three filament profiles, process settings and plate placement.
- Validation and reproduction details: [revision notes](v07_approved/README.md). Physical print: not tested.
