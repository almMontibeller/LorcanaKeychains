# Attack of the Vine changelog

## v07 — 2026-11-22

Redrew the title, punctuation, and curled vine from the original artwork. Replaced the three-filament gradient treatment with lime lettering and vine on purple. Rebuilt the purple silhouette around the new paths. Overall dimensions are 67.897 × 40.877 × 2.8 mm, compared with 68.010 × 40.905 × 2.8 mm in [v06](v06/Set_13_Attack_of_the_Vine_v06.3mf), the comparison and build baseline. The 2 mm backing, 6.5 mm bore and its position, and nominal 10.5 mm eyelet diameter carry over.

## v06

Compared with [v05](v05/Set_13_Attack_of_the_Vine_v05.3mf), raised the loop from 1.6 mm to the same 2 mm as the backing and widened the bore from 5.5 mm to 6.5 mm. The nominal 10.5 mm outer diameter gives a 2 mm radial rim. Bambu Studio sliced with all three filaments and zero plate warnings.

## v05

Compared with [v04](v04/Set_13_Attack_of_the_Vine_v04.3mf), made the loop 1.6 mm high with a stepped junction to the 2 mm backing. Bambu Studio sliced successfully and the toolpaths confirmed the lower loop.
