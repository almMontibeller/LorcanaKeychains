# Attack of the Vine changelog

## v1.6 — 2026-11-22

Redrew the title, punctuation, and curled vine from the original artwork. Replaced the three-filament gradient treatment with lime lettering and vine on purple. Rebuilt the purple silhouette around the new paths. Overall dimensions are 67.897 × 40.877 × 2.8 mm, compared with 68.010 × 40.905 × 2.8 mm in [v1.5](v1.5/Set_13_Attack_of_the_Vine_v1.5.3mf), the comparison and build baseline. The 2 mm backing, 6.5 mm bore and its position, and nominal 10.5 mm eyelet diameter carry over.

The historical entries below are reconstructed from the saved projects and reports. Physical print results are unknown for v1.0–v1.2 and unverified for v1.3–v1.5.

## v1.5

Compared with [v1.4](v1.4/Set_13_Attack_of_the_Vine_v1.4.3mf), raised the loop from 1.6 mm to the same 2 mm as the backing and widened the bore from 5.5 mm to 6.5 mm. The nominal 10.5 mm outer diameter gives a 2 mm radial rim. Bambu Studio sliced with all three filaments and zero plate warnings.

## v1.4

Compared with [v1.3](v1.3/Set_13_Attack_of_the_Vine_v1.3.3mf), made the loop 1.6 mm high with a stepped junction to the 2 mm backing. Bambu Studio sliced successfully and the toolpaths confirmed the lower loop.

## v1.3

Compared with [v1.2](v1.2/Set_13_Attack_of_the_Vine_v1.2.3mf), enlarged the original 4.5 mm bore to 5.5 mm. The loop and backing were both 2 mm high. Bambu Studio sliced with all three filaments and zero plate warnings.
