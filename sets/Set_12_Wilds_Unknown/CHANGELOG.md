## v1.7 · 2026-11-22

Compared with and built from [v1.6](v1.6/Set_12_Wilds_Unknown_v1.6.3mf).

- Traced 26 green accents across the 12 ivory letters. The 0.72 mm nominal strokes are flush with the ivory faces and occupy the top 0.40 mm, with 0.60 mm of ivory underneath. The backing, eyelet, side foliage, letter footprints and process settings carry forward from v1.6.
- Bambu Studio 2.9.1 sliced without plate warnings. Toolpaths contain all 26 connected accents on both inlay layers, with green confined to the drawn strokes. Physical print quality is unverified.

## v1.6 · 2026-11-22

Compared with and built from [v1.5](v1.5/Set_12_Wilds_Unknown_v1.5.3mf).

- Redrew all 12 letter faces and both foliage ornaments as Bézier curves traced from the archived artwork. Filled nine green flecks with ivory and kept the counters in D and O.
- Filled 24 backing openings around the lettering and blended the outer contour. The eyelet is the only through-hole. The footprint changed from 82.58 × 40.91 mm to 82.91 × 40.47 mm. The 2 mm backing and loop, 6.5 mm bore, 10.5 mm nominal eyelet and 3 mm overall height carry forward.
- Bambu Studio 2.9.1 sliced all three filaments with no plate warnings. The toolpath audit found a connected brown backing, six intended green foliage elements, and 12 separate ivory letters. Physical print status is unverified.
