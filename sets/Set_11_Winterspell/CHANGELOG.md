# Winterspell changelog

## v1.10 · 2026-11-22

Compared with and built from [v1.9](v1.9/Set_11_Winterspell_v1.9.3mf).

- Added six Ice Blue patches on both lower W tips, the N curl, both E feet and the shorter L’s crown. There are now thirteen patches. Gold remains the dominant colour on the letter faces.
- The model uses the flat 2.0 mm Dark Blue backing, 6.5 mm bore and 3.0 mm total thickness from v1.9.
- Bambu Studio sliced every letter and patch at full size with the 0.4 mm nozzle. Estimated print time is 23.6 minutes. Physical printing and fit remain unverified.

Compared with and built from [v1.6](v1.6/Set_11_Winterspell_v1.6.3mf).

## v1.6 · 2026-11-22

Compared with and built from [v1.5](v1.5/Set_11_Winterspell_v1.5.3mf), with the [original artwork](../../collection/v1.0/Sources/lorcana-keychains/sources/11.png) as the trace reference.

- Redrew the W and final L scrolls and all eleven letter bodies as smooth contours. The old ivory mesh had 17 separate pieces. The new mesh has one piece per letter.
- Replaced the jagged outline with a continuous Ice Blue field and Marine Blue backing. The backing now has one opening, the eyelet, down from four. Overall dimensions changed from 95.01 × 26.80 × 3.00 mm to 94.94 × 27.23 × 3.00 mm. The 6.5 mm bore and the three original filament slots carry forward.
- Bambu Studio sliced the model at full size with both the 0.4 mm and 0.2 mm nozzles, and at 110% XY with the 0.4 mm nozzle. All eleven letters stayed separate and connected on every ivory layer. Physical print and fit are unverified.
