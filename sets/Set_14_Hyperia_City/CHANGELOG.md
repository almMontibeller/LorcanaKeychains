# Hyperia City changelog

## v1.8 (2026-11-22)

Compared with and built from [v1.7](v1.7/Set_14_Hyperia_City_v1.7.3mf). Extended the Ice Blue strokes partway down the upper side contours, including H, C, I, and A. Tapered the ends and added inner-edge accents at the H curl, C, P, and R. The blue count rose from 14 to 18, with the same 71.90 × 40.56 × 3 mm dimensions and dark brown padding. Bambu Studio sliced the project without plate warnings and retained all accents, the letter openings, and the 6.5 mm bore. Physical printing is untested.

## v1.7 (2026-11-22)

Compared with and built from [v1.6](v1.6/Set_14_Hyperia_City_v1.6.3mf). Removed the Caramel padding so the ivory letters sit directly over dark Cocoa Brown. Moved Ice Blue to 14 accents along the upper letter edges, following the saved logo. The footprint remains 71.90 × 40.56 mm and the model reaches 3 mm at the blue accents. Bambu Studio sliced the three-colour project without plate warnings and kept the letters, accents, counters, and 6.5 mm bore clear. Physical printing is untested.

## v1.6 (2026-11-22)

Compared with and built from [v1.5](v1.5/Set_14_Hyperia_City_v1.5.3mf). Redrew all 11 letters and the backing, replaced the gradient with solid Ivory White, and restored Ice Blue as a continuous outline. The footprint changed from 71.92 × 40.79 mm to 71.90 × 40.56 mm. The project is 3 mm high with a 2 mm backing and eyelet and a 6.5 mm bore. Bambu Studio sliced it with zero plate warnings and retained the separate letters, the blue border, the three counters, and the open bore. Physical printing is untested.
