# Reign of Jafar v1.10

Open [Set_08_Reign_of_Jafar_v1.10.3mf](Set_08_Reign_of_Jafar_v1.10.3mf) as a Bambu Studio project. The A1 uses a 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers and Arachne walls. The red part’s top-surface and internal solid-infill widths change from 0.35 to 0.33 mm. The geometry, global settings, four filament slots and plate placement carry over from [v1.9](../v1.9/Set_08_Reign_of_Jafar_v1.9.3mf).

Bambu Studio sliced v1.10 with all four colours and no plate warnings. The strict toolpath audit found zero detected gaps through the red lettering layers at its 0.025 mm raster resolution. The cobra eyes, both red letter counters, gold outlines and all five engraved hood bands passed. The slicer estimates about 33 minutes.

The 0.2 mm nozzle trial with 0.10 mm layers also closed the lettering gaps. It took an estimated 2 hours 11 minutes and lowered the measured top eye coverage to about 97% per eye. The v1.10 0.4 mm slice gives full coverage in the same tested eye regions. Physical printing and chain fit are still to be checked.
