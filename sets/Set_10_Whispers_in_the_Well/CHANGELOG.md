## v1.6 — 2026-10-18

Compared with and based on [v1.5](v1.5/Set_10_Whispers_in_the_Well_v1.5.3mf).

- Replaced the rough raster colour trace with explicit closed Bézier contours for WHISPERS, IN THE, WELL, the gold upper wisp and grey lower rule. Simplified texture, chipped edges and fine hairlines; reinforced the first S's bowl and curl for better deposited coverage.
- Added one continuous Charcoal field beneath all letters and ornaments. Backing area increases from 1,304.53 to 1,641.65 mm², adding 337.12 mm² / 25.84%. The eyelet is the only remaining through-hole, down from 20 openings in v1.5.
- Overall envelope changes from 55.78 × 39.38 × 2.8 to 57.17 × 39.72 × 2.8 mm. Retained 2 mm backing and loop, a 7 mm eyelet neck, nominal 10.5 mm outside diameter, 2 mm radial rim, and every v1.5 6.5 mm bore coordinate and its centre.
- Kept the three original filament slots, profiles, process settings and placement. Both relief colours occupy Z = 2–2.8 mm without overlapping volumes.
- Validation: closed manifold meshes, no degenerate triangles, all artwork supported; successful Bambu slice with three colours and no plate warnings. Every relief layer has 10 connected grey features, 9 connected gold features and two clear letter counters. See [revision notes](v1.6/README.md) for coverage measurements and limits. Physical print: not tested.
