# Whispers in the Well v1.9

Open [Set_10_Whispers_in_the_Well_v1.9.3mf](Set_10_Whispers_in_the_Well_v1.9.3mf) as a Bambu Studio project. The charcoal backing below WELL is trimmed and the new bottom corners are rounded. The overall height in the plane drops from 39.72 to 35.83 mm, with 1.25 mm of charcoal below the lowest point of the W. The lettering, upper flourish, eyelet, and A1 / 0.4 mm / 0.20 mm Arachne profile carry over from [v1.8](../v1.8/README.md).

Bambu Studio 02.09.01.70 sliced all three colours without plate warnings. The toolpath audit confirmed the remaining lettering is fully supported and the former underline region is outside the printed footprint.
