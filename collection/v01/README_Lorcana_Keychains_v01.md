# Disney Lorcana binder keychains — v01

The designs and individual previews are now in `../../sets/<set>/v01/`.
This folder retains collection-wide documentation, reports, and historical source scripts.
The release notes below describe the original release.

Created 2026-10-16. Twelve personal-use binder labels for sets 3–14. Sets 1 and 2 are excluded as requested. Each new design was traced from the official English title artwork published by Disney/Ravensburger. The owner’s preliminary artwork files were not used. The accepted set 3 model is included unchanged, with a versioned filename.

## Printing

- Open a .3mf in Bambu Studio. These are unsliced multipart models, not printer projects.
- The message “The 3mf file has invalid config, load geometry data only” refers to the absence of Bambu-specific project settings. Click OK to import the model, then select your A1/nozzle/process presets.
- Keep each label assembled as one object. Assign its named parts to the corresponding AMS Lite filaments. Standard 3MF colour metadata may not automatically set Bambu AMS slots.
- Print flat back down, with the lettering facing up. No supports are intended. The back is 2.0 mm thick and raised art adds 0.4–1.0 mm. The integrated loop has a 4.5 mm hole and approximately 3 mm radial wall. A metal split ring is not included.
- Baseline: PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers, three walls and five top/bottom layers. For the finer frame lines and typography, especially sets 7, 9, 10, 11, 12 and 14, the 0.2 mm nozzle at 0.10 mm layers will preserve more detail. Inspect the slice preview for missing thin strokes.
- Geometry was checked for closed colour meshes and one connected assembled body, and re-imported from the exported 3MF. Models have not been physically test-printed or sliced in Bambu Studio.
- Gradients, glows, tiny textures and bevels are simplified into three or four solid filament colours. Preview colours are approximate; actual filament appearance will differ.
